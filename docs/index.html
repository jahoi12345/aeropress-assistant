AeroPress Assistant

A SwiftUI iPhone app that turns AeroPress recipes into a guided, timed brew, and remembers how each cup turned out.

  • 88 built-in recipes, including World AeroPress Championship podium recipes
  • Browse by equipment, cup type, brew method and accessories, or search
  • Step-by-step brew timer with per-step countdowns
  • Brew journal with ratings and tasting notes, plus a beans journal
  • Favorites, custom recipes and "I'm feeling lucky"
